- **[Adversarial Fast-Moving Real-World Domains as Test Beds for Benchmarking AI Scientist Capabilities](/publication/ai_scientist_testbeds/)**: Workshop on AI for Science. [Paper](/publication/ai_scientist_testbeds/ai_scientist_testbeds.pdf) · [Poster](/publication/ai_scientist_testbeds/poster.pdf)
